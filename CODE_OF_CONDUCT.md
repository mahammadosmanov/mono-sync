# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment or discrimination.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this.

Report violations privately to the maintainer at the contact address in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.
